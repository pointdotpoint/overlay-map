NightMap — how should the selected pin's info appear?

Centered panel (B) in hologram style. The crosshair is selecting a gig. Same info in each: type, name, district, threat, distance, and the actions you can take. Which presentation reads best while driving?

A — Tag at the crosshair

Compact label pinned next to the crosshair; moves with what you're pointing at. Minimal, but long text crowds the map.

B — Docked info card

Fixed card in the panel's right column with full details and actions. Always in the same place, never covers the selection.

C — Bottom info strip

One-line strip above the key hints, like a car nav's next-turn bar. Readable at a glance; less room for detail.
